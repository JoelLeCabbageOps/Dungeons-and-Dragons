
1. [[Sainhuin the Red Dragon]] attacks Mauervell, because there is a magic entity placed under the city by the completely well-meaning arch-mage to the king, Magnus.
2. The players cross paths in a court yard as the attack happens, and kobolds pour into the city by the thousands, not killing many but definitely attacking those who don't turn and run from them.
3. The players battle with some of these Kobolds and move toward a manor owned by friends of [[Ellary of Smiffen Burrows]] along with a number of civilians. The battle continues out the front door of this manor when the party realises there is a little girl back in the courtyard. They win and begin to flee the city to the outer wall, where thousands upon thousands have poured out.
4. A purple portal appears at the edge of the wide clearing outside the city walls, and soldiers from the enemy kingdom, Illystrial, come through with carts, chains, and a troll.
5. A spell is cast and all the folks who are not levelled individuals or otherwise mentally strong fall asleep in the presence of this spell. [[Tenet of Xervalon]] and [[Andrews]] resist the soldiers who come forward to take those awake in shackles. Tenet endures a natural 20 boulder throw from [[Eater of Men/Demetrius]] the Troll. They continue to resist for surprisingly long until they are subdued. [[Lucy Longbottom]] pretended to be asleep.
6. Taken prisoner, and walked for 10 days to the outer walls of [[Illystrial]] behind the carts full of ordinary civillians who fell asleep, [[Shailavar Merilas]] messed with the guards by pouring water on their heads and [[Vakama]] made smooth attempts to befriend guards and make himself more likeable.
7. The players move past a city they have been in for a one-shot, named [[Ladon]] on the way to Illystrial. At [[Illystrial]], the group is brought out from amongst the civilians and selected by a man named [[John Mani]], an inquisitor, to be his party.
8. John tells them they will be leaving the city the very evening he selected them, but they must first have tattoos placed on them. They objected, and he allowed them to have bracelets placed on their wrists.
9. They left, with the artificer, [[Ellary of Smiffen Burrows]] buying a buckler to serve as a shield for them.
10. Leaving the city they discussed the options and roles that an inquisitors party is subject to, such as proving themselves to be of some value to Illystrial by dungeoneering, finding historical artifacts, serving on the frontline battlefields against the orcs, etc. Normally an inquisitor would be strict about what their party would be doing, but John was eager to leave and give options to his party.
11. John taking charge told the party they would head south to a village, not telling the party they would be embarking on the same route he was taken on when he was in their position.
12. Their first location to visit was a one hour walk south to a village called [[Beyla]], a protected religious town with a strict non-interference policy given to them and similar small towns in the region. This tiny town had been turned to ants by a boy playing by a river who found a "lesser-wish tablet."
