Fort Luga's primary purpose is for Elves to keep record of the individuals travelling through to this side of the river. Nearly everyone is allowed through, and a small military force is kept here to ensure no groups of soldiers from other kingdoms come through.
### Population:

- Approximately 1500; mixed elf and halfling.

### Government:

- Fort Luga is governed by an order of knights and warriors, known as the Black Order.

### Notable Places:

- The Guildhall: A grand stone-walled building, decorated with stained glass windows. It contains a large meeting hall and several smaller rooms, and is shared amongst several local trade guilds.
- The Witch's Pub: A modest elven tavern, built around a hewn stone impaled by a sword.
- The Barracks: A buttressed building of timber and brick walls, headquarters of the town guard. It is built atop a small dungeon used to detain thieves and scofflaws.

### A few NPCs:

- Cosa: Female Halfling Illusionist, Good. Cosa is exceptionally beautiful, with golden hair and bright green eyes. She wears modest garments and wields a quarterstaff and dagger. Cosa is hunting the demon who murdered her family.
- Renza: Female Halfling Paladin, Good. Renza has blonde hair and brown eyes, and wears glasses with copper rims. She wears splint mail and wields a mace and shield. Renza has an animal companion, a bay warhorse named Lina.
- Cellio: Male Halfling Ranger, Good. Cellio is noble in bearing, with brown hair and blue eyes. He wears scale mail and wields a mace and dagger. Cellio is fascinated by legendary heroes and battles.
- Sava: Female Halfling Merchant, Evil. Sava has copper hair and blue eyes. She wears well-made clothing and carries a hawthorn staff. Sava is craven and autocratic.

### Quests: 
1. An elven lady named Indis seeks a company of adventurers to escort the Relic of Zeriel safely to the dwarven village of Bunala. Moreover, the party must complete the quest within 6 days.
2. A merchant named Berteph seeks a company of adventurers to find and explore the ancient ruins of Shestone Keep.
3. A merchant named Wene Bourne seeks a company of adventurers to slay the Serpent of Allarern and retrieve its eyes.
