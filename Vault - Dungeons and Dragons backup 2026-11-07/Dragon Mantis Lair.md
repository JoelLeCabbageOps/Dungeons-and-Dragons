The Dragon Mantis Lair takes place in a ~80 foot tall hill with cubed rooms extending out of the top, caused by the [[Heart of the dungeon]].
The primary entrance to this dungeon is hidden through a cave entrance that leads into the dungeon entrance.

### Entrance
1a. The cave entrance:
	While investigating the cave entrance, three giant mantis *without wings* approach the party from the tree line.
1b. The cave
	While entering the cave, a high passive perception or a good perception check spots 5 giant fire beetles approaching in the forest and 12 onlooking giant mantis. The inside of the cave is old and spider webs hang from the ceiling and damp moss covers every wall and ceiling of the cavern. And creature droppings are across the ground, along with balls of sticky clumps. (Mantis poo and vomit)
