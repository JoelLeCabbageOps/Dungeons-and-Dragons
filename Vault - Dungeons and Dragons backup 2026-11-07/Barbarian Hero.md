## 

_Medium humanoid (any), any alignment_

---

- **Armor Class** 16 (Breastplate)
- **Hit Points** 114 (12d12+36)
- **Speed** 40ft.

---

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|20 (+5)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|8 (-1)|

---

- **Saving Throws** STR +9, DEX +6, CON +7
- **Skills** Athletics +9, Perception +5
- **Damage Resistances** All, except for Psychic
- **Senses** Passive Perception 15
- **Languages** Common
- **Challenge** 11 (Defensive), 9 (Offensive)

---

**_Reckless._** The Barbarian has advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls, and attack rolls against it have advantage.

**_Feral Instinct._** The Barbarian has advantage on initiative checks, and can act on its turn even if it is surprised.

**_Relentless Rage._** When the Barbarian drops to 0 HP, it can make a DC 10 CON save. On a success, it drops to 1 HP instead. Each time it uses this feature, the DC increases by 5.

### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The Barbarian makes two attacks with its Greataxe. If it reduces a creature to 0 HP with one of these attacks, it may make a third Greataxe attack.

**_Greataxe, +2, GWM._** Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, one target. Hit: 25 (1d12 + 19) slashing damage.

On a critical hit, the Barbarian does 7 additional slashing damage.
