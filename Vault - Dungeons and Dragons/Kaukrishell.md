### Quests
1. She wants the party to visit a temple after signing an agreement that she gets old artifacts from an old dynasty building she thinks she found as a kid, but has found herself unable to talk about until the last few days. She thinks it's magic, but it's because she nearly died there. It is an actual dungeon though. An elf archer named Thucta seeks a company of adventurers to find and explore the ancient ruins of Arin's Deep. Before the end, the party discovers that her information is wrong.
2. A merchant named Beatry Moore seeks a company of adventurers to hunt down and kill the scattered servants of Mala Mackshaw. The quest is to keep an eye out in major towns, as they wear a particular eye shaped earings on their left ear.
3. An ancient aristocrat named Guiosa seeks a company of adventurers to escort a caravan of exotic goods safely to the elven village of Glanfon. It's a special fertlizer used in the mushroom farming the town relies on for healing potion and other such things.

### A few NPCs:

- Cela: Male Elf Artist, Neutral. Cela has a narrow face, with auburn hair and brown eyes. He wears modest garments and a silver holy symbol. Cela has an animal companion, a black cat named Dere.
- Breina: Female Elf Alchemist, Good. Breina is short, with red hair and grey eyes. She wears modest garments and numerous rings. Breina is lewd and lustful.
- Venda Ionurdotr: Female Dwarf Fighter, Evil. Venda has an angular face, with matted blonde hair and large brown eyes. She wears banded mail and wields a bastard sword and shield. Venda serves an ancient dragon named Abulaesh.

## Cobble Market

### Notable NPCs:

- Cora: Female Elf Bookseller, Neutral. Cora has cropped black hair and narrow hazel eyes, and a thin mouth. She wears modest garments and riding boots. Cora also deals in exotic inks and quills.
- Boudia: Female Elf Thief, Evil. Boudia is pleasant in appearance, with tangled auburn hair and blue eyes. She wears leather armor and wields a dagger and darts. Boudia is known for her short temper and sharp tongue.
- Essal: Female Elf Entertainer, Neutral. Essal has copper hair and brown eyes. She wears fine clothing and silk gloves. Essal is known for her generosity to beggars and waifs.
- Finnda: Female Elf Butcher, Evil. Finnda is tall, with white hair and large blue eyes. She wears modest garments and a pewter amulet. Finnda claims that her meats are blessed by Anael, Deity Pronoun of Beasts, and bestow strength.
- Feanarfinr: Male Elf Woodseller, Evil. Feanarfinr is haughty in bearing, with curly golden hair and light brown eyes. He wears modest garments and several pouches hang from his belt. Feanarfinr is known for his collection of magical axes.

## Drakespool Ward

### Notable Places:

- The Crossed Axes: A shabby commoner's tavern, said to be built atop a haunted catacombs.
- The Floating Alley: The ground between the buildings here appears to fall away into an empty void. A path of floating slates offers the only way across.

### A few NPCs:

- Ennad: Female Elf Ranger, Good. Ennad is pleasant in appearance, with grey hair and blue eyes. She chain mail and wields Corrupt Fury, a masterwork crossbow engraved with runes of vengeance. Ennad seeks to discover the truth of her lineage.
- Arfimin: Male Elf Necromancer, Evil. Arfimin is common in appearance, with red hair and narrow green eyes. He wears fine clothing and wields a short sword and dagger. Arfimin has a paralyzing fear of death.
- Coenwy: Male Human Ranger, Neutral. Coenwy has matted brown hair and bright hazel eyes, and numerous distinctive scars. He wears chain mail and wields a spear and javelins. Coenwy has a dire rat named Other.
