### Premise.

TLDR; Troll friend needs help and time and there's a big spider in the caves and some loot:
	In my world, trolls remain dumb and easily enraged creatures until they come in contact with a harsh and cold environment. When their hot mind has a moment to reach sufficiently low temperatures, they regain their exceedingly high intelligence. 
	When Boreas the troll found the cave, he was too hot and dumb, and the heart of the dungeon, reached out to his simple and enraged mind and claimed him as it's second protector, after the spider (boss mentioned below) in the dungeon. Once he regained his intelligence, he was no longer under it's sway, but still remained it's protector, somewhat immune to it even.

### Prelude.
[[Boreas the Troll]] is being savaged by kobolds using fire to prevent his healing, and as he is dismembered when the party arrives, with the ice pack that he wore on his head melting. Before he goes insane he implores the party for help, telling them the direction of his home and cave. All that's left of him is a head, and he will be fully regenerated by tomorrow. 
This dungeon is findable with a DC 18 survival check of the area, and evidence is followed through underbrush into the doorway that leads into the dungeon. With a check above a 12, footprints can be followed to the general area around the dungeon, and odd black and white patterned bugs float around a hive sitting in a tree. This hive contains the a satchel with an insignia from an order of knights called Thanatos Ippotis, or "Death Knights." Inside the papers contain magical maps that reveal little to the players other than a misdirection and their current location. It shows the neighbouring regions and a location where other Thanatos knights will be waiting to reclaim the belongings of their comrade, when the unsuspecting adventurers 'return' the belongings.

## Boss
- Large Ice Spider Queen
	- https://critterdb.com/#/bestiary/view/6a3f577715443bd5efcab0c1
	- She was previously a normal giant spider, but when she came into this dungeon looking for a home, the heart of the dungeon latched onto her, and claim her as it's effective avatar. Imbued with strength and elevated consciousness, she grew in size and became the first protector of the dungeon on behalf of the dungeon's heart.


First Level
	A long corridor with rooms, and dappled sunlight coming through the ceiling. Watery foot prints lead deeper down the tunnel, all the way through the doors and down the stairway to the second level.
	One of the rooms has bones and blood scattered around the inside. A high enough **investigation** check deduces that something has been eating here, otherwise they will simply see the bones and the knife on the chair.
Second Level
	Everywhere covered in webbing is either difficult terrain, or impassable to the general person and slowly needs cutting away at or a highly explosive spell, your choice.
	-
	The red crystals are explosive and with detonate once an attack roll is made on them, low AC and low HP. The biggest ones do 8d6 fire damage on a failed DC 13 dex save, and the smallest do 1d6 fire damage.
	-
	The sarcophagi each have an adventurer inside of them, overwhelmed and mind crushed, they are no longer themselves except for the left most one and the rightmost one. 
		The webbing sustains the creatures body and the spider queen controls them through an established connection with her silk. The guy in the leftmost one is a level 6 monk equivalent wearing a [[Ring of Mind Shielding]], and the rightmost one contains a deathknight. The ring of mind shielding stores his mind, and the deathknights armour replaces his injured flesh with metal beams, slowly. All of them except the left most one are aggressive when they open. sarcophagi 4-9 all burst at the same time when one of them is opened. Most of them are carrying standard weapons for their class and an explorers pack, but chuck a venom dagger in one of them and a nice wizards spell book in another one.
Third Level
	Filled with Boreas's belongings, there's a bed, a table with food, wardrobes filled with troll sized victorian era clothing, and his workshop for making a set of armour that will absorb the heart of the dungeon, turning it into a +3 set of plate armour that will grant fire immunity and keep the wearer absolutely freezing cold. But it's not ready yet and he needs another few days to finish it off.
