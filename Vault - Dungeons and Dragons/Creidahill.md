This towns main exports are potatoes and a logging industry.

### Population: inaccurate, use FTG

- Approximately 3000; primarily elf, some halfling.

### Government:

- Creidahill is governed by a court of aristocrats, led by a male elf named Pengalmalg.

### Notable Places:

- The Oculus of Night: A dome with a circular opening at its center, supported by a ring of caryatid columns. The sky seen through the oculus is perpetually dark, marked by strange constellations of stars.

### A few NPCs:

- Sena: Female Elf Craftsman, Neutral. Sena has copper hair and green eyes, and a pattern of distinctive marks on her neck. She wears worn clothing and several pouches hang from her belt. Sena has an animal companion, a green firedrake named Erwellan.
- Sterey: Male Halfling Alchemist, Evil. Sterey is tall and thin, with auburn hair and light hazel eyes. He wears expensive clothing and carries a fine stiletto. Sterey seeks to atone for past sins.
- Helry: Male Human Soldier, Good. Helry has curly blonde hair and large blue eyes. He wears leather armor and wields a bastard sword and shield. Helry blames trolls for every misfortune.
- Mhere: Male Elf Soldier, Good. Mhere is rough in appearance, with messy silver hair and amber eyes. He wears leather armor and wields a military pick and shield. Mhere refers to himself in the third person.

## Craftsman's Village

### Notable Places:

- The Garden of Statues: An overgrown garden filled with weirdly life-like stone statues. It is said that a medusa's lair lies somewhere within the hedge labyrinth.

### A few NPCs:

- Animloth: Female Elf Ranger, Good. Animloth has uneven grey hair and bright green eyes, and a tribal tattoo on her leg. She wears leather armor and wields a mace and javelins. Animloth is haunted by the memories of a past life.
- Alion: Male Elf Peasant, Neutral. Alion has thin brown hair and bright grey eyes. He wears modest garments and several small tools hang from his belt. Alion is cursed with amnesia, and is searching for the secret which will break the curse.
- Iocaic: Male Elf Fighter, Good. Iocaic has a round face, with messy auburn hair and blue eyes. He wears chain mail and wields a spear. Iocaic is a terrible liar.
- Lebredhien: Female Elf Alchemist, Good. Lebredhien is fey in appearance, with grey hair and brown eyes. She wears fine clothing and a sling of vials and potions. Lebredhien believes that the gods are actually ancient and powerful dragons.
- Palard: Male Halfling Fighter, Good. Palard has grey hair and brown eyes, and a small mouth. He wears banded mail and wields a fauchard-fork. Palard seeks to help the poor and unfortunate.

## Hawk's Market

### Notable NPCs:

- Thuctu: Female Elf Peddler, Neutral. Thuctu has auburn hair and amber eyes. She wears travel-stained clothing and carries a yew staff. Thuctu sometimes has a magical trinket to sell.
- Ellalwen: Female Elf Entertainer, Evil. Ellalwen is haughty in bearing, with golden hair and soft amber eyes. She wears modest garments and several pouches hang from her belt. Ellalwen is also known as the best knife-thrower in town.
- Fila: Male Elf Thief, Neutral. Fila is short and slender, with tangled brown hair and narrow green eyes. He wears leather armor and wields a long sword and dagger. Fila was once an adventurer, but retired after Illfang the Kobold Lord murdered his companions.
- Celure: Male Elf Butcher, Neutral. Celure has brown hair and sharp green eyes. He wears worn clothing and several pouches hang from his belt. Celure is known for his chimera sausages, made from various exotic meats.
