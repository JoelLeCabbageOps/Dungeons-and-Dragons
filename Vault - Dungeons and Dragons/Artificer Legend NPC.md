## Artificer Legend

_Medium humanoid (any), any alignment_

---

- **Armor Class** 23 (Half Plate +2, Shield +2)
- **Hit Points** 165 (17d8 + 85)
- **Speed** 30ft.

---

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8 (-1)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|20 (+5)|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|

---

- **Saving Throws** STR +5, DEX+8, CON +17, INT +17, WIS +6, CHA +7
- **Skills** Perception +6
- **Senses** Passive Perception 16
- **Languages** Common
- **Challenge** 15 (13,000 XP)

---

**_Fortified Position._** The Artificer and its allies within 10 feet have half cover.

### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The Artifcer attacks once with its Firebolt, and twice with its Force Ballista. It may replace its Firebolt attack with another spell.

**_Firebolt._** Spell Attack: +11 to hit, reach 120 ft., one target. Hit: 27 (4d10+1d8) fire damage.

**_Force Ballista._** Spell Attack: +11 to hit, reach 120 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d8) force damage and the target is pushed 5 feet back.

**_Cone of Cold (1/day, 5th level spell)._** 60 ft cone, DC 19 CON save. On a failed save, the target takes 41 (9d8) cold damage, or 1/2 damage on a success.

**_Fireball (6/day, 3rd level spell)._** 60 ft range, 20 ft radius sphere, DC 18 DEX save. On a failed save, the target takes 33 (8d6+1d8) fire damage, or 1/2 damage on a success.

### Reactions

**_Shield._** As a reaction to being targeted with an attack, the Artificer may add 5 to its AC until the beginning of its next turn.

**_Flash of Genius (5/day)._** As a reaction to seeing an ally within 30 feet make a saving throw, the Artificer can add +5 to it.

**_Soul of Artifice (6/day)._** As a reaction to dropping to 0 HP, the Artificer may choose to drop to 1 HP instead. The Artificer takes a -1 penalty to saving throws for each time it has used this ability today.
