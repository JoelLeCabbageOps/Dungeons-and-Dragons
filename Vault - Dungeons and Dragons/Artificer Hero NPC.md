### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The Artifcer attacks once with its Firebolt, and once with its Force Ballista. It may replace its Firebolt attack with another spell.

**_Firebolt._** Spell Attack: +9 to hit, reach 120 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d10+1d8) fire damage.

**_Force Ballista._** Spell Attack: +9 to hit, reach 120 ft., one target. Hit: 14 (3d8) force damage and the target is pushed 5 feet back.

**_Fireball (3/day, 3rd level spell)._** 60 ft range, 20 ft radius sphere, DC 17 DEX save. On a failed save, the target takes 33 (8d6+1d8) fire damage, or 1/2 damage on a success.

### Reactions

**_Shield (4/day)._** As a reaction to being targeted with an attack, the Artificer may add 5 to its AC until the beginning of its next turn.

**_Flash of Genius (5/day)._** As a reaction to seeing a friendly creature within 30 feet make a saving throw, the Artificer can add +5 to it.
