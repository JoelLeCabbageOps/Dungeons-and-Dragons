**_Stunning Strike (5/day)._** When the monk hits a creature with a melee attack, it can force the creature to make a DC16 CON save or be stunned until the end of the Monk's next turn.

**_Evasion._** When the monk makes a Dexterity saving throw, it takes no damage on a success, and half damage if it fails.

### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The Monk attack four times with its Unarmed Attack, and once with either its Shove or its Trip. It may replace two of its Unarmed Attacks with a single Dodge, Dash, or Disengage action.

**_Unarmed Attack._** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit. Hit: 10 (1d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

**_Shove._** Target creature must make a DC 16 Strength save or be pushed back 15 feet.

**_Trip._** Target creature must make a DC 16 Dexterity save or be knocked prone.

**_Stillness of Mind._** The Monk ends one effect on itself causing it to be charmed or frightened.

### Reactions

**_Deflect Missiles._** When the monk is hit with a ranged weapon attack, it may use its reaction to subtract 23 (1d10+17) from the damage. If this reduces the damage to 0, it may make a ranged attack with a range of 20/60 using the ammunition it just caught as part of the same reaction. This attack has +9 to hit and deals 1d8+5 piercing damage.
