### Quests
1. Upon entering the town, an ethereal lady opens up the orb that [[Black Phoenix]] is laying in, inhabiting her with permission from [[Black Phoenix]] with the quest to free them both from their prisons.
2. An arrogant aristocrat named Tersa seeks a company of adventurers to hunt down and kill Kas the Bloody. Moreover, the party must complete the quest before a rival party does.
3. The season right now is summer, and the summer wolves around the town have massively overpopulated this season. Adventuring parties can take on the assignment to bring back 10 summer wolf pelts at a time as the usual hunters are too scared of getting burned to death, which has already happened to some of the young men.
