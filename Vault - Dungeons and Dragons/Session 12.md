[[Altair]] stops to engage the party, telling them they match the description of people he has been sent to find and assist.

[[Misthaven under Quenya]]: 
As the town widens through the trees, it looks fit to support a population of a few thousand. Green tiled roofs and wood and brick houses stand before you. A small sign down the road says "Misthaven". Large Elvish men with saws, shovels, and logs are milling in the open and digging holes. They appear to be placing large stakes in the ground. In response to any questions they will say "Since the attack on Mauervell, all Quenyan towns have been asked to take procautions against overeager kobolds."

Entering the town, it moves around freely, and ahead of you is a courtyard filled taller buildings that looks like inns and eateries. A small sign says "Upper Market"
